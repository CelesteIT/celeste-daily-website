About Celeste Hope

Celeste Hope is created as part of our commitment to community care, awareness, and responsible social impact.

Through this initiative, Celeste aims to create a simple and accessible platform to share awareness content, campaign updates, support messages, and important information related to selected cancer areas. This page will be updated further with detailed campaign information, visuals, partner details, and official awareness content.

Selected Cancer Awareness Areas

The campaign will focus on six selected cancer awareness areas. The detailed content can be updated later once finalized.

Area 01

Breast Cancer

Awareness on early detection, regular checks, and community education.

Area 02

Cervical Cancer

Sharing awareness on prevention, screening, and timely medical guidance.

Area 03

Oral Cancer

Promoting awareness about risk factors, warning signs, and early care.

Area 04

Lung Cancer

Creating awareness on prevention, symptoms, and the importance of support.

Area 05

Colorectal Cancer

Encouraging awareness about screening, lifestyle factors, and early diagnosis.

Area 06

Childhood Cancer

Supporting awareness, compassion, and care for children and families.
